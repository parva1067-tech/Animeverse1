NARUTO

ABOUT THE ANIME

The Way of the Ninja

Naruto follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage of his village. Despite being treated as an outsider, Naruto continues to train, make friends and overcome powerful enemies.

His journey is filled with friendship, rivalry, sacrifice and the determination to never give up.

GENRE Action / Adventure / Drama / Fantasy / Shonen
WORLD Hidden Leaf Village
THEME Never Give Up

MAIN CHARACTERS

THE RIVAL

A talented shinobi driven by his complicated past.

THE LEGEND

Itachi Uchiha

A highly skilled shinobi whose story is full of sacrifice.

The Great Pirate Adventure

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy and his Straw Hat crew as they travel across the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the One Piece.

Their adventure brings them face-to-face with powerful pirates, Marines and mysterious islands while building unforgettable bonds.

GENRE Adventure / Fantasy / Action / Shonen / Comedy / Fantasy
WORLD Grand Line
THEME Freedom

MAIN CHARACTERS

A powerful swordsman aiming to become the world's greatest.

Sanji

Protect the living.

ABOUT THE ANIME

The World of Soul Reapers

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and becomes responsible for protecting humans from dangerous spirits.

His journey takes him into the mysterious Soul Society and eventually into conflicts involving powerful enemies and Soul Reapers.

GENRE Action / Supernatural / Drama / Shonen
WORLD Soul Society
THEME Protection

MAIN CHARACTERS

Warriors of the Soul Society

PROTAGONIST

Ichigo Kurosaki

A human who becomes a Soul Reaper and protects the people around him.

SOUL REAPER

Rukia Kuchiki

A Soul Reaper whose encounter with Ichigo changes both their lives.

ANTAGONIST

Sosuke Aizen

A brilliant and calculating Soul Reaper with enormous power.
